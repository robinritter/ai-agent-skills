Section 10 must include next event and horizon total, recipients, tokens, USD,
percentage of current circulation, conflicts and coverage. Section 11 separates
fees, revenue, tokenholder flows, incentives and disclosed costs. Section 17
explains peer fit and dilution. Do not call a low unit price cheap.

For projects under 30 days, emphasize what cannot yet be observed. For projects
over two years, compare delivery/adoption against dated promises. Age alone proves
neither quality nor failure. Explain technical terms before relying on them.
